## Deferred Scope

These areas remain outside v1.0 unless promoted into a later milestone:

- Production handoff: layered source packages, print preflight, PDF/PSD/AI-style handoff, installer notes, scale, bleed, DPI, and color workflow.
- True 3D preview: verified vehicle models, UV/material slots, camera presets, material finish simulation, and screenshot/export consistency tests.
- Advanced generation and orchestration: masks, inpainting, ControlNet/IP-Adapter-style inputs, multi-view consistency, and multi-agent orchestration after typed pipeline artifacts are proven.
- Business and community: quote/order/payment flows, collaborator sharing, moderated galleries, remixing, and broad licensed template/asset libraries.
- Hosted provider production rollout: provider quality, pricing, moderation, account access, quota behavior, and commercial-rights constraints need current validation before non-local generation is enabled.
